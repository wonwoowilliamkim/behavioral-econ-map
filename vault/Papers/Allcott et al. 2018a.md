# Ramsey Strikes Back: Optimal Commodity Taxes and Redistribution in the Presence of Salience Effects

**Allcott, Lockwood, Taubinsky · 2018 · AEA Papers and Proceedings**

**Section:** §5.5 Consumption vs. Income Taxes

## Abstract

Shows Atkinson-Stiglitz theorem breaks down with non-salient commodity taxes. Optimal commodity taxes follow Diamond (1975) Many-person Ramsey rule. Differential commodity taxes become useful even when income taxation is available.
