# How Do Individuals Repay Their Debt? The Balance-Matching Heuristic

**Gathergood, Mahoney, Stewart, Weber · 2019 · AER**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20180288)
**Section:** Household Finance

## Abstract

Analyzes credit card repayment behavior for millions of UK accounts. Documents that consumers use a balance-matching heuristic — splitting payments proportionally to card balances rather than directing payments to the highest-interest card. This simple rule is normatively sub-optimal (costing hundreds of dollars per year) but highly robust across consumer types and credit market conditions.
