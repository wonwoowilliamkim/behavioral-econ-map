# The Short-Run and Long-Run Effects of Behavioral Interventions: Experimental Evidence from Energy Conservation

**Allcott, Rogers · 2014 · AER**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.104.10.3003)
**Section:** §3.5 Nudges

## Abstract

Evaluates Opower social comparison nudges for energy conservation. Finds effects persist but decay over time. Estimates welfare effects of the nudge using willingness-to-pay elicitation.
