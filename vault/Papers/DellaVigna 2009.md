# Psychology and Economics: Evidence from the Field

**DellaVigna · 2009 · JEL**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/jel.47.2.315)
**Section:** Overview

## Abstract

Comprehensive survey of field evidence on behavioral economics: limited attention, non-standard beliefs, and non-standard preferences. Organizes findings around deviations from the standard model and evaluates their policy implications. Gateway reference for the field.
