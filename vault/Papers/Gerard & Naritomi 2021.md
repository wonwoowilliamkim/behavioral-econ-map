# Job Displacement Insurance and (the Lack of) Consumption-Smoothing

**Gerard, Naritomi · 2021 · AER**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20190388)
**Section:** Insurance

## Abstract

Uses Brazilian administrative data to show that displaced workers experience large consumption drops despite generous severance pay and UI benefits. Workers spend most severance immediately rather than spreading it over the unemployment spell. Provides evidence that liquidity constraints and present bias jointly undermine consumption smoothing after job loss.
