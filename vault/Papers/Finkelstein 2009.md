# E-ZTAX: Tax Salience and Tax Rates

**Finkelstein · 2009 · QJE**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1162/qjec.2009.124.3.969)
**Section:** §3.4 Tax Salience

## Abstract

Electronic toll collection (E-ZPass) reduces tax salience, leading governments to raise toll rates. Natural experiment showing that salience affects the political economy of taxation.
