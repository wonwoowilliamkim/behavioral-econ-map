# A Method for Evaluating the Quality of Financial Decision Making

**Ambuehl, Bernheim, Lusardi · 2017 · NBER Working Paper 20618**

**Section:** §4.6 Financial Education

## Abstract

Introduces financial competence measure based on WTA discrepancies for equivalent claims. Shows standard financial education increases tested knowledge but not decision quality—motivational rhetoric drives behavioral change, not substance.
