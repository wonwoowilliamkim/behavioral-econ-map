# The Welfare Effects of Nudges: A Case Study of Energy Use Social Comparisons

**Allcott, Kessler · 2019 · AEJ: Applied Economics**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/app.20170328)
**Section:** Non-Standard Policy Instruments

## Abstract

Combines Opower energy nudge data with willingness-to-pay elicitation to compute welfare effects. Finds the nudge is welfare-improving but imprecisely targeted: it helps the unbiased as well as the biased, and the WTP for the nudge is on average positive but heterogeneous.
