# Heterogeneity, Demand for Insurance, and Adverse Selection

**Spinnewijn · 2017 · AEJ: Economic Policy**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/pol.20140254)
**Section:** Insurance

## Abstract

Studies adverse selection when consumers have heterogeneous beliefs and risk preferences. Distinguishes between "type" variation (risk aversion, risk level) and "mistake" variation (biased beliefs about risk). Shows that corrective insurance subsidies can be welfare-improving when biased beliefs drive selection.
