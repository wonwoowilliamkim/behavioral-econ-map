# Smoke Gets in Your Eyes: Cigarette Tax Salience and Regressivity

**Goldin, Homonoff · 2013 · AEJ: Economic Policy**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/pol.5.1.302)
**Section:** Tax Misperceptions

## Abstract

Studies differential salience of cigarette excise taxes (included in shelf price) vs. sales taxes (added at register) across income groups. Low-income smokers are more responsive to the salient excise tax than the non-salient sales tax; high-income smokers respond similarly to both. Tax salience varies by socioeconomic status, affecting both the behavioral response and the distributional incidence of cigarette taxes.
