# Optimal Sin Taxes

**O'Donoghue, Rabin · 2006 · JPubE**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpubeco.2006.01.006)
**Section:** §3.2 Corrective Taxation

## Abstract

Studies taxation of unhealthy foods under present-bias. Shows optimal tax is positive whenever present focus causes overconsumption and consumers are price-sensitive. General application of the modified Pigouvian principle.
