# Unemployed but Optimistic: Optimal Insurance Design with Biased Beliefs

**Spinnewijn · 2015 · JEEA**

**Section:** §5.6 Social Insurance

## Abstract

Modifies Baily-Chetty optimal unemployment insurance formula for biased beliefs about reemployment prospects. Slope effect (return to search) vs. level effect (job-finding probability) have different policy implications.
