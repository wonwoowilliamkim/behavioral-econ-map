# A Macroeconomic Approach to Optimal Unemployment Insurance: Theory

**Landais, Michaillat, Saez · 2018 · AER**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20150233)
**Section:** Insurance

## Abstract

Extends the Baily-Chetty optimal UI formula to a macroeconomic model with matching frictions. Adds a job-creation externality: UI raises workers' outside options, stimulating job creation by firms. In slack labor markets, the optimal replacement rate exceeds the partial-equilibrium optimum. Provides a new sufficient statistic for optimal UI that depends on the labor market tightness.
