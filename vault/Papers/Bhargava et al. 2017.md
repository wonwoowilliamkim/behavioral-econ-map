# Choose to Lose: Health Plan Choices from a Menu with Dominated Options

**Bhargava, Loewenstein, Sydnor · 2017 · QJE**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjx011)
**Section:** Insurance

## Abstract

Shows 61% of employees choose dominated health insurance plans. The magnitude of welfare losses is large (hundreds to thousands of dollars). Employees with more education and actuarial training make significantly better choices. Motivates simplification of health insurance menus.
