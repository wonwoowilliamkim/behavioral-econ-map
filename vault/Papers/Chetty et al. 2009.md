# Salience and Taxation: Theory and Evidence

**Chetty, Looney, Kroft · 2009 · AER**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.99.4.1145)
**Section:** §3.4 Tax Salience

## Abstract

Field experiment + natural experiment showing consumers underreact to taxes not included in posted prices (σ ≈ 0.06–0.35). Derives efficiency cost formula showing underreaction reduces deadweight loss through two channels.
