# Golden Eggs and Hyperbolic Discounting

**Laibson · 1997 · QJE**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1162/003355397555253)
**Section:** §2.2.5 Time-Inconsistency; §4 Saving

## Abstract

Popularizes the β-δ (quasi-hyperbolic discounting) model. Shows that illiquid assets serve as commitment devices for present-biased consumers. Foundational paper for the self-control literature in behavioral economics.
