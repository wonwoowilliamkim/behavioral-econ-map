# Evaluating Behaviorally-Motivated Policy: Experimental Evidence from the Lightbulb Market

**Allcott, Taubinsky · 2015 · AER**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20140261)
**Section:** §3.2 Corrective Taxation

## Abstract

Within-subject experiment on lightbulb purchases. Debiasing intervention reveals consumer undervaluation of energy costs. Derives price-metric bias γ and computes optimal corrective subsidy using the internality framework.
