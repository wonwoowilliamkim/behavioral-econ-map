# A Simple Mapping from MPCs to MPXs

**Laibson, Maxted, Moll · 2021 · NBER Working Paper 29323**
[↗ Full paper](https://www.nber.org/papers/w29323)
**Section:** Household Finance

## Abstract

Derives analytical mappings from marginal propensities to consume out of income (MPCs) to marginal propensities to spend out of illiquid wealth (MPXs) in a model with present bias and illiquid retirement assets. Shows that present bias amplifies the household balance-sheet channels of fiscal and monetary policy. Provides micro-foundations for the high MPCs documented in household data.
