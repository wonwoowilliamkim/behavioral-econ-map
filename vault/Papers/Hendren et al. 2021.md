# Choice in Insurance Markets: A Pigouvian Approach to Social Insurance Design

**Hendren, Landais, Spinnewijn · 2021 · Annual Review of Economics**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-economics-082019-110739)
**Section:** Insurance

## Abstract

★ STARRED. Develops a Pigouvian framework for social insurance that accounts for behavioral frictions in insurance choice. Distinguishes between private and social valuations of insurance. Shows corrective subsidies are welfare-improving when behavioral frictions cause under-insurance.
