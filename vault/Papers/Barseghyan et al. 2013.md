# The Nature of Risk Preferences: Evidence from Insurance Choices

**Barseghyan, Molinari, O'Donoghue, Teitelbaum · 2013 · AER**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.103.6.2499)
**Section:** Insurance

## Abstract

Estimates structural risk preferences from household deductible choices across home and auto insurance lines. Finds that probability distortions — overweighting small loss probabilities, as in prospect theory — are more important than diminishing marginal utility in explaining observed choices. Heterogeneity in probability weighting is substantial and policy-relevant.
