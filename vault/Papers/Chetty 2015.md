# Behavioral Economics and Public Policy: A Pragmatic Perspective

**Chetty · 2015 · AER**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.p20151108)
**Section:** §2 Behavioral Welfare Economics

## Abstract

Advocates for the sufficient statistics approach: use reduced-form estimates of behavioral wedges to evaluate policy without fully specifying cognitive models. Frames decision utility vs. experienced utility as alternative welfare foundations.
