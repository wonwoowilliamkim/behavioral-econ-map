# Optimal Taxation with Behavioral Agents

**Farhi, Gabaix · 2015 · NBER Working Paper 21524**

**Section:** §3 Commodity Taxation; §5 Income Taxation

## Abstract

General framework for optimal taxation with behavioral agents. Derives formulas for both commodity and income taxes that generalize standard sufficient statistics by adding price-metric bias terms. Shows Ramsey-style formulas re-emerge with non-salient commodity taxes.
