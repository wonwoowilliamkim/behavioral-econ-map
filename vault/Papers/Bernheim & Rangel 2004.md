# Addiction and Cue-triggered Decision Processes

**Bernheim, Rangel · 2004 · AER**

**Section:** §3.3 Distributional Concerns; §4 Saving

## Abstract

Models addiction as cue-triggered hot states. Argues welfare-relevant domain excludes choices made in triggered states using neurobiological evidence. Derives the surprising result that optimal tax on addictive goods may be negative.
