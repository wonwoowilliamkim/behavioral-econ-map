# RCTs to Scale: Comprehensive Evidence from Two Nudges Units

**DellaVigna, Linos · 2022 · Econometrica**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.3982/ECTA18709)
**Section:** Non-Standard Policy Instruments

## Abstract

Analyzes 126 RCTs run by two large government nudge units. Academic-published nudges have 8× larger effect sizes than unpublished trials — publication bias explains much of the "nudge optimism." Provides calibration for real-world effectiveness of behavioral interventions at scale.
