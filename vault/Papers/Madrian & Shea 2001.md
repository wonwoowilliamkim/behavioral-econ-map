# The Power of Suggestion: Inertia in 401(k) Participation and Savings Behavior

**Madrian, Shea · 2001 · QJE**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1162/003355301753265543)
**Section:** §4.5 Default Options

## Abstract

Seminal paper documenting dramatic default effects in 401(k) enrollment. Auto- enrollment increases participation rates from ~49% to ~86%. First rigorous evidence of status quo bias in retirement savings.
