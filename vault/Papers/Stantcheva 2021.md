# Understanding Tax Policy: How Do People Reason?

**Stantcheva · 2021 · QJE**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjab033)
**Section:** Perceptions of Public Policy

## Abstract

★ STARRED. Uses large-scale survey experiments to study how people reason about tax policy. Finds respondents use "mental frameworks" that shape policy views independently of standard economic logic. Provides new approach to measuring policy perceptions at scale.
