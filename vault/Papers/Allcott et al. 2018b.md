# Regressive Sin Taxes, with an Application to the Optimal Soda Tax

**Allcott, Lockwood, Taubinsky · 2018 · QJE (forthcoming)**

**Section:** §3.3 Distributional Concerns

## Abstract

Estimates optimal soda tax accounting for both internalities and externalities. Develops framework for optimal tax when sin goods are regressively consumed. Finds moderate tax justified despite regressivity when corrective benefits dominate.
