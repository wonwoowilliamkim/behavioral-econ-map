# (Over)insuring Modest Risks

**Sydnor · 2010 · AEJ: Applied Economics**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/app.2.4.177)
**Section:** Insurance

## Abstract

Documents that homeowners systematically choose low deductibles for home insurance despite actuarially unfavorable premium-deductible trade-offs. The implied risk aversion required to rationalize choices is implausibly high (coefficients of relative risk aversion > 100). More plausible explanations include loss aversion and probability overweighting of modest, predictable losses.
