# Taxpayer Confusion: Evidence from the Child Tax Credit

**Feldman, Katuscak, Kawano · 2016 · AER**

**Section:** §5.3 Tax Misperceptions

## Abstract

RD design showing taxpayers confuse lump-sum transfers with marginal tax changes. Loss of CTC associated with decline in reported wage income, even though CTC is lump-sum and should not affect labor supply incentives.
