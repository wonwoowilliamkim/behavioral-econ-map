# Is Addiction 'Rational'? Theory and Evidence

**Gruber, Koszegi · 2001 · QJE**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1162/003355301753265570)
**Section:** §3.2 Corrective Taxation

## Abstract

Incorporates quasi-hyperbolic discounting into the rational addiction model. Derives optimal cigarette tax that corrects both externalities and internalities from self-control failures. Key application of the modified Pigouvian principle.
