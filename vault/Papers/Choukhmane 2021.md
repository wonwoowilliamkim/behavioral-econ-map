# Default Options and Retirement Saving Dynamics

**Choukhmane · 2021 · Working Paper**
[↗ Full paper](https://tahachoukhmane.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Choukhmane_Defaults.pdf)
**Section:** Non-Standard Policy Instruments

## Abstract

Studies how default contribution rates in Danish retirement plans shape saving dynamics over the long run. Finds that defaults have large persistent effects due to inertia, but workers do eventually adjust — the "bite" of the default fades over time. Derives optimal dynamic defaults that front-load nudge strength early in workers' careers, yielding substantial welfare gains relative to static default policies.
