# Borrowing to Save? The Impact of Automatic Enrollment on Debt

**Beshears, Choi, Laibson, Madrian, Skimmyhorn · 2022 · JF**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13182)
**Section:** Household Finance

## Abstract

Tests whether automatic 401(k) enrollment causes liquidity-constrained workers to substitute retirement saving for consumer debt. Using military personnel data, finds that auto-enrollment increases retirement savings without significant increases in non-retirement debt. Workers do not simply borrow to offset forced saving — the default raises net wealth, supporting the welfare case for nudge-based retirement policy.
