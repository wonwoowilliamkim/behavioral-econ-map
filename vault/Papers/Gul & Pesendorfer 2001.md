# Temptation and Self-Control

**Gul, Pesendorfer · 2001 · Econometrica**

**Section:** §2.2.2 BRP; §4 Saving

## Abstract

Axiomatizes temptation preferences where consumers experience a penalty when choosing anything other than the most tempting option. Accounts for choice reversals without time inconsistency.
