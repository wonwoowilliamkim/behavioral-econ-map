# Private Information and Insurance Rejections

**Hendren · 2013 · Econometrica**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.3982/ECTA10489)
**Section:** Insurance

## Abstract

Shows that private information about health risk causes large-scale insurance market unraveling: applicants who are rejected for coverage are precisely those with the most private knowledge of their high risk. Develops an empirical test for private information using rejection rates and subsequent mortality. Quantifies the welfare cost of private information through market exclusion.
