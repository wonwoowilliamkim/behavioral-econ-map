# Regulating Consumer Financial Products: Evidence from Credit Cards

**Agarwal, Chomsisengphet, Mahoney, Stroebel · 2015 · QJE**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qju037)
**Section:** Household Finance

## Abstract

Evaluates the CARD Act of 2009 (credit card regulation) using a regression discontinuity design. Finds the Act reduced fees by $11.9 billion per year with minimal offsetting increases in interest rates. Provides causal evidence that consumer financial protection regulation can improve welfare.
