# Adverse Selection and Inertia in Health Insurance Markets: When Nudging Hurts

**Handel · 2013 · AER**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.103.7.2643)
**Section:** Insurance

## Abstract

★ Boot camp key paper. Documents large inertia in health insurance plan choice. Reduced-form and structural evidence that inertia generates adverse selection: inertia keeps healthy individuals in generous plans. Eliminating inertia reduces welfare through adverse selection even as it improves choice quality.
