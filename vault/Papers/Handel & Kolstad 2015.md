# Health Insurance for 'Humans': Information Frictions, Plan Choice, and Consumer Welfare

**Handel, Kolstad · 2015 · AER**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20131126)
**Section:** Insurance

## Abstract

★ Boot camp key paper. Combines revealed preference data with a survey on plan knowledge to separate information frictions from risk preferences in health insurance choice. Shows that information frictions explain much of the apparent risk aversion. Information provision substantially improves welfare.
