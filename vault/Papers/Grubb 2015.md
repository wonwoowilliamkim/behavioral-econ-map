# Overconfident Consumers in the Marketplace

**Grubb · 2015 · JEP**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/jep.29.4.9)
**Section:** Behavioral IO

## Abstract

★ STARRED. Reviews the literature on overconfident consumers in product markets. Firms exploit consumer overconfidence through shrouded add-on fees, unexpected charges, and complex pricing. Market competition does not eliminate exploitation. Policy interventions (disclosure, mandated simplicity) can improve welfare.
