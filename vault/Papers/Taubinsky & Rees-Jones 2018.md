# Attention Variation and Welfare: Theory and Evidence from a Tax Salience Experiment

**Taubinsky, Rees-Jones · 2018 · REStud**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/restud/rdx069)
**Section:** Tax Misperceptions

## Abstract

★ STARRED. Runs a tax salience experiment with heterogeneous attention. Shows that attention to taxes varies substantially across consumers, and that ignoring this heterogeneity biases welfare calculations. Derives optimal tax formula accounting for attention variation.
