# Behavioral Hazard in Health Insurance

**Baicker, Mullainathan, Schwartzstein · 2015 · QJE**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjv029)
**Section:** §5.6 Social Insurance

## Abstract

Derives optimal copay formula for health insurance with behavioral consumers. Biases can cause over-use of some medications and under-use of others. Extends behavioral public economics framework to health insurance design.
