# Measuring the Welfare Effects of Shame and Pride

**Butera, Metcalfe, Morrison, Taubinsky · 2022 · AER**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20200751)
**Section:** Non-Standard Policy Instruments

## Abstract

Develops a method to measure the welfare effects of social image concerns (shame and pride) in behavioral interventions. Uses a within-subject design to elicit WTP for avoiding shame from charitable giving. Shows shame and pride have substantial and heterogeneous welfare effects that standard analyses miss.
