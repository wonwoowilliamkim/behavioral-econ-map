# Are High-Interest Loans Predatory? Theory and Evidence from Payday Lending

**Allcott, Kim, Taubinsky, Zinman · 2022 · REStud**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/restud/rdab066)
**Section:** Household Finance

## Abstract

Develops a framework to evaluate payday lending regulation with behavioral consumers. Uses a within-subject experiment to measure biased beliefs about loan rollovers. Finds meaningful internalities from over-optimism about repayment. Optimal policy depends on whether the goal is correcting biases or reducing prices.
