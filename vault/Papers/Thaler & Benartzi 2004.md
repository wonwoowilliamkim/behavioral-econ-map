# Save More Tomorrow™: Using Behavioral Economics to Increase Employee Saving

**Thaler, Benartzi · 2004 · JPE**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1086/380085)
**Section:** §4.5 Default Options

## Abstract

Introduces the SMarT (Save More Tomorrow) program: employees commit in advance to automatically increase their 401(k) contribution with each pay raise. Exploits present bias and inertia. Participation rates triple relative to control. Highly influential in policy design.
