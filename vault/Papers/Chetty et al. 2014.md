# Active vs. Passive Decisions and Crowd-Out in Retirement Savings Accounts

**Chetty, Friedman, Leth-Petersen, Nielsen, Olsen · 2014 · QJE**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qju013)
**Section:** §4.5 Default Options

## Abstract

Uses Danish administrative data to show 401(k) contributions from automatic employer contributions do not crowd out active saving, while subsidies primarily shift saving across accounts. 85% of people are "passive savers." Implications for optimal retirement savings policy design.
