# Beyond Revealed Preference: Choice-Theoretic Foundations for Behavioral Welfare Economics

**Bernheim, Rangel · 2009 · QJE**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1162/qjec.2009.124.1.51)
**Section:** §2 Behavioral Welfare Economics

## Abstract

Proposes the Bernheim-Rangel framework for welfare analysis without relying on revealed preference. Introduces the unambiguous choice relation P* as the unique welfare criterion satisfying three basic properties. Foundation of modern behavioral welfare economics.
