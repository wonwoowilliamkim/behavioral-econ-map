# Energy Policy with Externalities and Internalities

**Allcott, Mullainathan, Taubinsky · 2014 · JPubE**

**Section:** §3.2 Corrective Taxation

## Abstract

Studies optimal policy mix (energy tax + appliance subsidy) when consumers underweight future energy costs. Key result: optimal energy tax is below the Pigouvian level because standard consumers over-respond to it.
