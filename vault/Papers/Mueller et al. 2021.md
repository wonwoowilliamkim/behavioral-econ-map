# Job Seekers' Perceptions and Employment Prospects: Heterogeneity, Duration Dependence, and Bias

**Mueller, Spinnewijn, Topa · 2021 · AER**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20190896)
**Section:** Insurance

## Abstract

Measures job seekers' subjective beliefs about reemployment using survey data and compares them to realized outcomes. Documents systematic overoptimism, especially among long-term unemployed. Beliefs update negatively with duration in ways inconsistent with Bayesian updating. Implications: correcting biased beliefs could improve search efficiency; optimal UI must account for heterogeneous and biased agents.
