# Equilibrium Effects of Food Labeling Policies

**Barahona, Otero, Otero, Kim · 2021 · Econometrica**

**Section:** Non-Standard Policy Instruments

## Abstract

Studies Chile's mandatory warning labels on unhealthy foods (the "black octagon" policy). Combines scanner data with a structural model to identify equilibrium effects including firm reformulation responses. Labels reduce unhealthy purchases and trigger substantial product reformulation.
