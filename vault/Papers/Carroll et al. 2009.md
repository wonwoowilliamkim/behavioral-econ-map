# Optimal Defaults and Active Decisions

**Carroll, Choi, Laibson, Madrian, Metrick · 2009 · QJE**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1162/qjec.2009.124.4.1639)
**Section:** §4.5 Default Options

## Abstract

Derives optimal 401(k) default contribution rate. Under long-run welfare criterion, sufficiently low β justifies active choice requirements rather than passive defaults. Shows opt-out maximization can be welfare-improving.
