# Reference-Dependent Job Search: Evidence from Hungary

**DellaVigna, Lindner, Reizer, Schmieder · 2017 · QJE**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjx025)
**Section:** Insurance

## Abstract

Uses administrative data from Hungary to show job search effort and re-employment hazard follow reference-dependent patterns. A spike at UI benefit exhaustion is consistent with loss aversion around a reference wage. Structurally estimates a reference-dependent job search model.
