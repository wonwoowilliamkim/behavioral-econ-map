# Moral Hazard versus Liquidity and Optimal Unemployment Insurance

**Chetty · 2008 · JPE**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1086/588585)
**Section:** Insurance

## Abstract

Disentangles moral hazard from liquidity effects in unemployment insurance. Shows that most of the consumption-smoothing benefit of UI comes from liquidity, not moral hazard reduction. Implication: optimal UI is higher than traditional models suggest.
