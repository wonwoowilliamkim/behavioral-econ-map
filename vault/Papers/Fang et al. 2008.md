# Sources of Advantageous Selection: Evidence from the Medigap Insurance Market

**Fang, Keane, Silverman · 2008 · JPE**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1086/597192)
**Section:** Insurance

## Abstract

Documents advantageous selection in Medigap: healthier individuals buy more supplemental insurance, opposite to adverse selection predictions. Cognitive ability is the key omitted variable — high-ability individuals are both healthier and more likely to purchase supplemental coverage. Shows that multi-dimensional heterogeneity can reverse the direction of selection in insurance markets.
