# Psychological Frictions and the Incomplete Take-Up of Social Benefits

**Bhargava, Manoli · 2015 · AER**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20121493)
**Section:** §5.7 Tax Filing

## Abstract

Field experiment on EITC take-up. Simplified notices dramatically increase take-up. Psychological frictions—not stigma—are the main barrier. Addresses the incomplete take-up puzzle for redistributive programs.
