# Choice Inconsistencies among the Elderly: Evidence from Plan Choice in the Medicare Part D Program

**Abaluck, Gruber · 2011 · AER**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.101.4.1180)
**Section:** Insurance

## Abstract

Reveals systematic choice inconsistencies in Medicare Part D drug plan selection. Enrollees overweight premiums relative to expected out-of-pocket costs and exhibit status quo bias. Implies substantial welfare losses from suboptimal plan choice among the elderly.
