# Revealed Preference Analysis with Framing Effects

**Goldin, Reck · 2020 · JPE**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1086/706767)
**Section:** Non-Standard Policy Instruments

## Abstract

Extends revealed preference welfare analysis to account for framing effects. Introduces the concept of "frame-invariant preferences" and characterizes when observed choices identify welfare even in the presence of framing. Bridges the Bernheim-Rangel framework and revealed preference.
