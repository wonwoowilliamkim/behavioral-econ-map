# Poverty and Self-Control

**Bernheim, Ray, Yeltekin · 2015 · Econometrica**

**Section:** §4 Saving

## Abstract

Formalizes internal self-control through history-dependent equilibria. Shows external commitments can undermine internal self-regulation. Low initial assets trap people in poverty by limiting self-control strategies.
