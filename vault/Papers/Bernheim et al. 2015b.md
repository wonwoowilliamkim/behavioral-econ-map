# The Welfare Economics of Default Options in 401(k) Plans

**Bernheim, Fradkin, Popov · 2015 · AER**

**Section:** §4.5 Default Options

## Abstract

Applies Bernheim-Rangel framework to 401(k) defaults. Shows EVA and EVB bounds on welfare effects lead to similar optimal defaults for contribution rates below 20%. Default is generally insensitive to the decision frame in this range.
