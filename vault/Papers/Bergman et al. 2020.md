# Creating Moves to Opportunity: Experimental Evidence on Barriers to Neighborhood Choice

**Bergman, Chetty, DeLuca, Hendren, Katz, Palmer · 2020 · NBER Working Paper**
[↗ Full paper](https://opportunityinsights.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/cmto_paper.pdf)
**Section:** Inequality

## Abstract

★ STARRED. RCT offering housing search assistance + financial support to voucher-holding families in Seattle. Creates Moves to Opportunity (CMTO) treatment triples the share of families moving to high-opportunity neighborhoods. Shows behavioral barriers — search frictions, limited information — are the key obstacle, not preferences.
