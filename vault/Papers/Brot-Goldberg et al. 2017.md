# What Does a Deductible Do? The Impact of Cost-Sharing on Health Care Prices, Quantities, and Spending Dynamics

**Brot-Goldberg, Chandra, Handel, Kolstad · 2017 · QJE**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjx013)
**Section:** Insurance

## Abstract

Studies a large employer-mandated shift to high-deductible health plans using administrative claims. Finds consumers reduce spending bluntly across all care categories — including high-value preventive care — rather than targeting low-value spending. Implies that HDHPs impose welfare costs by inducing consumers to forgo valuable care, not just wasteful care.
