# The Social Determinants of Choice Quality: Evidence from Health Insurance in the Netherlands

**Handel, Kolstad, Minten, Spinnewijn · 2021 · NBER Working Paper**

**Section:** Insurance

## Abstract

★ STARRED. Examines how socioeconomic factors predict health insurance choice quality using Dutch administrative data. Lower-income, less-educated households make systematically worse plan choices. Social determinants of choice quality have strong implications for corrective insurance policy design.
