# Behavioral Industrial Organization

**Heidhues, Koszegi · 2018 · Handbook of Behavioral Economics**

**Section:** Behavioral IO

## Abstract

Comprehensive handbook chapter reviewing how consumer biases shape firm strategy and market equilibrium. Covers shrouded add-on pricing (firms hide true costs), naivete about future usage (cell phones, credit cards), and switching costs with present-biased consumers. Shows that market competition does not protect naive consumers — firms can extract more surplus from biased consumers than from rational ones. Reviews policy interventions including disclosure mandates and fee regulation.
