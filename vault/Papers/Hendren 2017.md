# Knowledge of Future Job Loss and Implications for Unemployment Insurance

**Hendren · 2017 · AER**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20151655)
**Section:** Insurance

## Abstract

Estimates the degree to which workers have private information about their future job loss risk using expenditure and income data. Finds substantial private information that generates adverse selection in UI. Derives upper and lower bounds on the optimal UI replacement rate accounting for this selection, showing that optimal UI may be significantly lower than Baily-Chetty when private information is severe.
