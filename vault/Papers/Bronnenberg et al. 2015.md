# Do Pharmacists Buy Bayer? Informed Shoppers and the Brand Premium

**Bronnenberg, Dube, Gentzkow, Shapiro · 2015 · QJE**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjv024)
**Section:** §3.2 Corrective Taxation

## Abstract

Pharmacists and doctors buy generic drugs more than laypeople. Uses expert choices as rational consumer benchmark to measure bias from imperfect information. Application of the rational consumer benchmark welfare method.
