# Evolving Choice Inconsistencies in Choice of Prescription Drug Insurance

**Abaluck, Gruber · 2016 · AER**
[↗ Full paper](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20130778)
**Section:** Insurance

## Abstract

Extends the 2011 Medicare Part D analysis to examine how choice quality evolves as enrollees gain experience. Finds that choice inconsistencies persist and inertia is the dominant driver of suboptimal plan selection over time. Policies mandating active re-enrollment significantly improve welfare by forcing annual reconsideration.
