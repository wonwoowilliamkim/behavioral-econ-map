# The Good, the Bad, and the Ugly: A Unified Approach to Behavioral Welfare Economics

**Bernheim · 2016 · Journal of Benefit-Cost Analysis**

**Section:** §2 Behavioral Welfare Economics

## Abstract

Refines the Bernheim-Rangel framework. Distinguishes direct vs. indirect judgments, argues that behavioral economics challenges indirect but not direct judgments. Provides revised premises for choice-oriented welfare analysis.
