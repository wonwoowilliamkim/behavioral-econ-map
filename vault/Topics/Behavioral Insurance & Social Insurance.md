# Behavioral Insurance & Social Insurance

How behavioral frictions — inertia, inattention, biased beliefs, adverse selection — shape insurance markets and social insurance programs. Covers health insurance choice, unemployment insurance design, and the welfare cost of behavioral frictions in insurance markets.
